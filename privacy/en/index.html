A Lovely Day Privacy Policy

This policy explains how the "A Lovely Day" app (Korean name: 예쁜하루, the "App"), made by an individual developer, Kim Mucheol (the "Developer"), handles your information.

The Developer does not run any server and does not receive or keep the information you enter. Everything you enter stays on your own device.

Morning reminder: if you turn it on in Settings, the App calculates today's fortune on your device around 8 AM in your device's time zone and shows it as a notification. Nothing is sent off the device for this. Notification permission is requested when you turn it on, and you can turn it off anytime.

Sharing: tapping the share button sends an image with the date, score, title and summary to the app you choose. Your name and birth info are not included. What happens afterwards follows that app's policy.

3. International transfers

AdMob, Google Play Billing and In-App Review, and Android location search are operated by Google LLC (United States). The information in the table above may be transferred to Google's servers in the United States and other countries when you use these features, and retention follows Google's privacy policy. Birth dates sent for calendar conversion go to the Korea Astronomy and Space Science Institute in the Republic of Korea.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Ads and your choices

Where required (for example in the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and certain US states), the App asks for your consent through Google's consent tool before showing ads. You can change your choice anytime under "Ad privacy choices" in Settings, and you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. You can keep using the App even if you decline personalized ads.

The Developer does not sell your personal information. Any sharing with Google for advertising happens only as described above and according to the choices you make.

5. Retention and deletion

  • Information on your device is kept until you tap Delete birth info in the App or uninstall the App, and is then removed from the device immediately.
  • The App does not use automatic backup, so nothing remains after changing devices or reinstalling.
  • Ad removal and theme purchases stay with your Google Play account and can be applied again with "Restore purchases".

6. Your rights

You can view, change or delete your birth info anytime in the App's Settings. Because the Developer keeps no copy outside your device, there is no other data held by the Developer to access, correct, export or erase. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or the CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to processing of your data, and to complain to your local data protection authority. For data processed by Google, you can use the tools described in Google's privacy policy. You can also contact the Developer at the email below.

7. Cookies and similar technologies

The App does not use cookies. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may use your advertising ID for ads; you can reset or delete it in your device settings.

8. Security

  • Birth info is stored only in the App's private storage, which other apps cannot read.
  • Support emails do not automatically include your birth date or time (only the app version and Android version).

9. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 where local law requires a higher age), and the Developer does not knowingly collect their information.
